# Research

> **Status: research, not product.** Nothing in this directory is published to npm, covered by the release process, or supported. Numbers here describe a market snapshot on a given date and are meant to be re-run, not relied on. Packages that exist only to test a paper's claims are marked `private` and say so in their README.

| Study | What it measures | Reference implementation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`sparse-settlement/`](./sparse-settlement) — *When Not to Settle Probabilistically: Sparse Settlement and Regime Selection for x402* | The x402 Bazaar index split by payer recurrence; where a probabilistic ticket pays once merchant variance, buyer exposure, the client's per-payment cap and Permit2 onboarding bound it; the cost of choosing one settlement scheme for every resource | [`packages/sparse`](../packages/sparse) — rail on `createRail()`, in-memory commit–reveal facilitator, the §6 regime selector, and `SparseSettlementProxy.sol` with a gas benchmark against the real Permit2 bytecode; passes the rail conformance suite | Draft v0.9 (exact binomial variance bound). Second index snapshot scheduled 2026-10-21 for the out-of-sample test |

How to read a study: the paper draft is `paper-v*.md`; scripts take `SNAPSHOT=` and reproduce every table; `snapshots/` is git-ignored and regenerated by the fetch script. If a study's conclusion changes on re-measurement, the draft gets a new version, not an edit.
